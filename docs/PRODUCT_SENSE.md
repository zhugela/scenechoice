# Product Sense

> What the product is for, who it serves, and what "good" looks like. Detailed specs live in `docs/product-specs/`.

## Who it serves

The owner is building this as both a product and a learning vehicle — to genuinely master Python, FastAPI, AI-agent engineering, testing and defense, toward an ~15K RMB AI-application-developer role in Shenzhen. Every module must ultimately be understood, modified, explained and defended by the owner, not just generated.

## The differentiation

Not "call an LLM". The promise is a **story that is guaranteed to be finishable**:

- one line in, a full 3/5-episode blueprint out;
- deterministic state changes (rules), model only narrates;
- every ending is reachable and non-identical;
- bad model output never reaches the player — it is repaired or rejected.

## What "good" means for an M1 blueprint

A blueprint passes only if, beyond structure:

- it has a beginning, a conflict, a turn, and an ending;
- the two states are relevant to the theme;
- the two choices produce genuinely different consequences (not the same effect with different wording);
- endings are reachable and not three variants of the same path.

Structure <= quality; the 20/20 structural eval does not prove the stories are fun. That is why human story scoring is part of the M1 gate.

## Non-goals for now

DB, frontend, RAG, multi-agent, community/login/payment, free-text player input. Keep the loop short until the gate passes.
